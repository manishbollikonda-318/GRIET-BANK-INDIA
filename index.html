🏛️ Core OOP Principles Used

  • Encapsulation: Private balances guarded by deposit() & withdraw().
  • Inheritance: SavingsAccount & CurrentAccount extend BankAccount.
  • Polymorphism: Overridden withdraw() enforcing per-transaction caps.
  • Abstraction: Abstract BankAccount with abstract contract methods.
